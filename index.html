WATERING MISSION

Smart Garden Watering Assistant

This prototype provides digital watering-session tracking and reminders. It does not directly detect or control the physical bore motor.

Users manually start the digital session when they physically begin watering and manually check the motor when alerted.

This is a software prototype developed as a college project to demonstrate smart reminder-based watering habits.

Features
  • ⏱️ Session timing & countdown
  • 🔔 Sound alerts & notifications
  • 📊 Watering history tracking
  • 📅 Weekly 3-session goal planner
  • 💾 Persistent local data storage
